How I Installed Arch Linux

Harder Than I Thought • August 3, 2025

Day Zero: Why Arch?

Arch Linux had always felt like some rite of passage. Minimal, powerful, completely DIY. I wanted to learn the Linux internals the hard way. Turns out, the hard way was... harder than I thought.

No GUI installers. No hand-holding. Just you, a terminal, and a wiki that assumes you already have a PhD in Linuxology.

Preparing the Ritual

  • Flashed the ISO using dd
  • Prayed to the TTY gods
  • Booted into the void

Everything looked like the Matrix. Except more intimidating. And with worse font rendering.

The Installation Gauntlet

Partitioning? Check. Mounting? Check. Pacstrap? Cool word, confusing tool.

I spent more time in the Arch Wiki than actually installing things. Every step had sub-steps. Every sub-step had caveats. One wrong move and I was staring into a black void again.

The Breakthrough

After two reinstallations, I finally booted into a functional system. No DE, just a TTY. I installed i3, set up networking, and made it pretty — the rice was real.

My terminal looked clean. My fonts were nerdy. I had risen from the ashes... a little traumatized but a lot more knowledgeable.

"Installing Arch isn’t about proving you're a hacker. It’s about becoming one."

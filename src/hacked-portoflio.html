How I Hacked OverTheWire

by Sunny • August 3, 2025

The First Encounter

So I stumbled across OverTheWire — a legendary wargame site for learning Linux and hacking basics. Bandit was the first challenge. I was hooked faster than a reverse shell on an open port.

It's not flashy. No gamification. Just you, your terminal, and the cold hard truth of the command line. Let's go.

Weapons of Choice

  • ssh for accessing remote servers
  • cat, ls -la, and file for snooping
  • find and strings for the real dirty work

What I Learned (a.k.a. Got Humble Real Quick)

Some passwords are hidden in plain sight. Some are in weird files like .hidden. Some are inside binaries pretending to be harmless.

But it’s not about the answers. It’s about getting comfy navigating the CLI, reading error messages, Googling like a pro, and realizing that yes, tab completion is your best friend.

The Hacker Aesthetic™

I couldn’t just play Bandit in a plain terminal. So I customized mine with neon green fonts, translucent black backgrounds, and a rain animation behind it all — Matrix vibes, fully deployed.

Was it overkill? Probably. Did I feel like the main character in a cyberpunk anime? Absolutely.

"Learning to hack isn’t about breaking the rules. It’s about understanding them better than anyone else."
